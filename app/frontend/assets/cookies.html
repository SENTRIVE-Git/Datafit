Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 2026
Short version: DataFit does not set any cookies. We audited the site's actual code to confirm this before writing this page, rather than assuming it and writing generic cookie-policy boilerplate. There's no cookie banner on this site because there's genuinely nothing to consent to.

1. Cookies we set

None. DataFit does not use first-party cookies for authentication, preferences, analytics, or any other purpose. We currently don't have user accounts or saved preferences, so there's nothing that would require a cookie yet.

2. Third-party requests that aren't cookies, but are worth disclosing anyway

The site loads two typefaces from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com). This is a direct network request from your browser to Google's servers, not a cookie — but since it does involve a third party receiving your IP address as a technical side effect of loading the font files, we're disclosing it here for full transparency rather than only in the Privacy Policy.

ResourceProviderPurposeSets cookies?
Web fonts (Archivo Black, Courier Prime)Google FontsSite typographyNo

3. If this changes

If we ever add analytics, advertising, or any technology that does set cookies, this page will be updated first, and a proper consent mechanism will be added before that technology goes live — not retroactively.

4. Related pages

See our Privacy Policy for how we handle uploaded datasets, and our Terms of Service for the terms of using DataFit.
